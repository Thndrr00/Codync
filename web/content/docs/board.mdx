The board keeps the tasks for one computer on one screen: four columns and a card for each task.
Open it from the checklist icon in the rail (desktop). The host keeps the cards, so every window of
that computer sees the same board, and it is still there after a restart.

## Columns

- **To do:** not started.
- **Doing:** in progress.
- **Waiting:** on someone else, or on a decision.
- **Done:** finished, kept in its column instead of disappearing.

Each card's menu moves it to another column or deletes it; a card can also be dragged from one column
to another. A card can name the bot that owns the task; its name and face sit on the card, and clicking
them opens that bot's chat, where the work itself happens. A due date, when set, turns red once it has
passed and writes one reminder a day in that bot's chat when it is close.

A card in **Doing** can also name one of its bot's routines: when that routine's next run finishes, the
card moves to **Done** if it succeeded or **Waiting** if it failed. **Run its routine now** in the card's
menu starts that run. The board asks the bot's routines in the add row once a bot is picked.

**Export CSV** saves the cards you can see (title, status, bot, due date, repeat and detail); **Clear
done** removes every finished card after asking.

The search box filters cards by their title and detail, and the picker beside it shows one bot's cards.

## Adding a card

Type the task in **New card**, pick a bot if one owns it, and choose **Add**. A card is a note for
you: the board does not start a turn by itself. Ask the bot in its chat (or set up a routine) and move
the card as the work moves.

A card's menu also has **Edit card…** for its title, a longer detail, a due date and a repeat (once,
every day or every week); finishing a repeating card puts the next one in **To do** with its due date
moved on. **Add to the board** in any message's menu puts that message on the board as a card.

## From anywhere

`Ctrl+Shift+B` (`Cmd+Shift+B` on a Mac) brings Codync forward on the board, whatever app you are in;
in the message box, `/task <title>` adds a card without leaving the chat.

## More than one computer

If the app is connected to several computers, the picker at the top chooses whose board is shown. Each
computer has its own board; cards are not synced between them.
