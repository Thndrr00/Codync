A bot can build a small web app you can open in a tab: a board, a form, a dashboard. Codync serves it in a sandbox and keeps its data on your computer. The bot can keep improving it from your messages.

## Ask the bot for one

In the bot's chat:

> Build me a board to track these tasks, with a page I can open.

The bot saves the app under a name and gives you a card in the chat. Click **Open** to use it. The card carries the version, so when the bot publishes a fix the open tab reloads.

Apps run in an isolated origin: they cannot read your Codync cookies, local storage or other tabs. Data they keep goes to the bot's own collections on your computer, not to an outside service.

## Share an app

A bot's app card has **Share link**. Make a link that can **view** or **edit**, then send it to someone. A view link can read the app and its data; an edit link can change them. Sharing never needs the other person to sign in to Codync. You can revoke a link from the app's list.

## Public forms

A bot can make an app a form. It names which collections the public may write to (`submit`) and which it may read (`read`), then shares a **submit** link. Someone who opens it can add an item, but cannot change or delete anything, and cannot read other people's submissions. Each submission gets a private receipt they can use to read back just their own entry.

Ask the bot for one:

> Make a sign-up form where people can add their name, and give me a public link.

## Ask the bot a question with choices

When a bot needs a decision, it can put a card in the chat with two to four options:

> Which database should I use?

Tap an option and the answer goes straight into the bot's chat as your next message. The bot carries on from your choice. Nothing you type into the app or the card is used for analytics.

## Limits

An app runs only on the computer its bot lives on. Your phone reaches it through Codync's encrypted channel, so it works even when you are away, but a link stops working if that computer is offline. Apps are small tools, not a replacement for a deployed website: there is no custom domain, account system or public hosting.
