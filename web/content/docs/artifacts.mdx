## What an artifact is

An artifact is a page a bot makes for you to read as a whole or come back to: a report, a plan, a comparison, a tracker or a dashboard. The bot writes it as one HTML file, so it can have tables, charts and scripts of its own. Ask for one ("make me a page that tracks my applications"), and bots also make one on their own when a long answer reads better as a page.

## In the chat

An artifact shows in the bot's chat as a card: the top of the artifact, its title and **Artifact**. Click or tap the card to open it.

The card always shows the newest version, even an older card from before the bot changed it.

## Opening an artifact

The artifact opens full size over the chat:

- **Close artifact** (the X) goes back to the chat. On the desktop, each artifact you open from a card or the Library gets its own tab over the chat: keep several open, go between them and close them one by one or all at once.
- The title at the top renames it: click or tap it, type the new name (**Rename artifact**).
- **Share artifact**: on the desktop, **Save as HTML** saves the file and **Open in browser** opens it in your default browser. On the iPhone, the share sheet gives you `<title>.html`.
- Links in an artifact open in your browser. Its scripts run on their own, apart from Codync, and it keeps its own saved state (a tracker's ticks stay ticked).
- **Terminal UI:** pick the card (`v`), then `o` opens it in your browser and `f` saves `<title>.html` to Downloads.

An artifact can be up to 5 MB.

## Diagrams

A diagram is an artifact you can edit. Ask for a flowchart, a plan or a sketch, or a bot makes one when a picture explains it better. It appears as a **Diagram** card; open it on the desktop to move shapes, add rectangles, ellipses, diamonds, arrows and text, change colors, and choose **Save diagram**. The saved diagram reopens as you left it, and the bot can change it when you ask.

Diagrams use the Excalidraw scene format, so the JSON can move between Codync and Excalidraw. The iPhone and Android apps open the card and say to edit it on the desktop; they don't draw the scene yet.

A diagram can be up to 2 MB.

## Changing an artifact

The box at the bottom of an open artifact, **Message about “title”**, sends the bot a message about this artifact. The bot changes the same artifact instead of making a new one; the open artifact reloads with the new version, and every card for it shows it.

You can also reply to the artifact's card in the chat; it reaches the bot the same way.

An artifact a bot made during a routine run has no card in the chat, so there's no box: ask about it in the chat.

## The Library

Each bot's **Library** holds what was made or shared in its chat, newest first under **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week** and **Earlier**:

- its artifacts, which open as above; the menu on a row also has **Rename artifact** and the share actions,
- files the bot shared, which download like their card in the chat,
- files and images you sent; an image opens full size.

Where to find it:

- **Desktop:** click the bot's name at the top of the chat, then the **Library** tab.
- **iPhone:** tap the bot's name at the top of the chat, then the **Library** tab.
- **Terminal UI:** press `L` for the bot's Library tab. `Enter` or `o` opens an artifact in your browser, `f` saves any row to Downloads, and `r` renames an artifact.

The Library looks through the bot's newest 500 messages with files.

## The server's Library

The server's **Library** gathers every bot of one computer in the same view: their artifacts and the files they shared or you sent, newest first under the same days. It reads the same stores as each chat's Library, so nothing on your disk is scanned, and a file forwarded to two bots shows once.

**Images** shows only pictures (PNG, JPEG, HEIC, GIF, WebP, TIFF, BMP) and **Files** everything else, including artifacts. Each row names the bot it came from; a click opens it the way the chat does (an artifact in its viewer, a picture full size, a file's download or share sheet).

Where to find it:

- **Desktop:** **Library** in the window's icon bar, or type "Library" in the search palette. **Refresh** reads it again.
- **iPhone:** the book button beside the roster's filters, or **Server Library** on a chat's Library page.
- **Android:** **Library** among the roster's actions, or **Server Library** on a chat's Library page.
- **Terminal UI:** `Ctrl+K`, then **Library of every bot: artifacts and files…**. `Tab` changes the filter, `Enter` opens an artifact in your browser or a file's text in the sheet (an image says how to save it), `f` saves a row to Downloads, and `r` renames an artifact.

Artifacts are kept on your computer, in Codync's data folder. Deleting the bot deletes them.

## Cards Codync draws

Some things a bot shows you are better as a small card than as a page: a number with its change, a plan beside another plan, what happened in order, a short table, a few steps to tick, or a short form. Codync draws those itself, from six named cards: **metric**, **comparison**, **timeline**, **table**, **checklist** and **form**.

A card is a name and its content, checked by your computer before anything is drawn. It can't carry HTML, a script or a link, so a card from any bot, on any of your computers, looks the same and is safe to show. Ask for one ("show me a table of my routines"), and a bot may make one on its own when a list reads better as a card. A **form** card returns what you fill in to the bot.

### The Components page

**Components** in the desktop window's icon bar shows every card Codync can draw, with a preview, the pieces of content each takes, and which computers' data it can read. Each card has a switch per bot: turn it off and that bot can't show that card any more. Saved cards — ones you made in the playground — sit under the six, and can be shown in the gallery or taken out of it.

The **Playground** tab makes a card: pick one, write its content in a form, check it, preview it, and save it as a draft or straight into the gallery. The content is all plain text and numbers; the computer refuses anything else and names the field to fix.

Where to find it:

- **Desktop:** **Components** in the window's icon bar, or type "Components" in the search palette.
- **iPhone and Android:** **Components** in the computer's settings: the six cards with a preview, and the switch that withholds one from a bot. Making a card is the desktop's job.
- **Terminal UI:** `Ctrl+K`, then **Components: the cards Codync can draw in a chat…**. `↑`/`↓` moves, `r` reads it again, `q` closes.
